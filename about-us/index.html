Everything you need to know about Poonia Movers' credentials, pricing, and operations

Poonia Movers is an ISO 9001:2015 certified logistics company (Govt. GST Registered: 08AALCP4432K1Z9) and an IBA-approved transportation partner. With 15+ years of verified industry experience since 2011, we operate our registered head office at Shop-23A, Harmada, VKI, Jaipur - 302013 with 100% legal compliance and transit insurance coverage.

Poonia Movers provides GPS-enabled transport vehicles including Tata Ace, Bolero Pickup, 14ft Eicher, 19ft closed container, and 32ft multi-axle trucks. Unlike aggregator apps like Porter or local commission brokers, our trained permanent crew handles packing, loading, transport, and delivery with direct carrier accountability.

We operate on a transparent 0% advance booking commitment for all Jaipur local shifting and intercity relocations. You pay ₹0 at the time of slot confirmation. 80% is payable upon safe loading at your origin, and the final 20% balance is settled only after 100% undamaged delivery, room placement, and your final inspection.

Our shifting operations utilize an engineered 5-layer protective packaging system comprising virgin air bubble wrap (80 GSM), high-density corrugated sheets, heavy-duty stretch film wrap, custom corner edge protectors, and waterproof heavy-gauge tarpaulins for complete weatherproofing and shock absorption during transit.

We provide 100% declared value transit insurance underwritten by national insurers (coverage up to ₹10 Lakh+). In the rare event of transit accidental loss or damage, claims are documented immediately upon unloading with a guaranteed resolution protocol processed within 48 to 72 hours.

Since our founding in 2011, Poonia Movers has successfully executed over 32,500+ household moves, 2,800+ corporate office relocations, 14,000+ car & bike shipments, and over 1,20,000 tonnes of commercial freight deliveries across all 33 districts of Rajasthan and 28 Indian states.

Our Jaipur local shifting charges start from ₹3,000 to ₹4,500 for 1 BHK, ₹5,500 to ₹8,500 for 2 BHK, and ₹9,500 to ₹14,000 for 3 BHK homes. Intercity long-distance relocations start from ₹12,000 to ₹38,000 depending on mileage and vehicle container size with guaranteed zero hidden costs.
