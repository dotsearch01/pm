Beware of Fake Packers & Movers

Moving fraud is real — fake companies take advances and disappear, or hold goods hostage for extra money. Here is how to protect yourself.

10 Warning Signs of a Fake Mover

  1. Demands 50–100% advance before moving day (we take 0% advance)
  2. No physical office address — only a mobile number and a free website
  3. No GST invoice or bilty offered — everything is "cash only"
  4. Quote far below market — then doubles the price on moving day
  5. Refuses a written quote — "trust me, madam/sir" instead of paperwork
  6. No company branding on truck, uniforms or documents
  7. Fake reviews — dozens of 5-star reviews posted within days
  8. Copies a famous brand name with a small spelling change
  9. No insurance option at all
  10. Pressures you to decide "today only"

Verification Checklist (2 minutes)

  • ✔ Ask for GSTIN and verify it on the GST portal
  • ✔ Visit or video-call their physical office
  • ✔ Demand a written quote on letterhead with itemised charges
  • ✔ Check Google reviews — read the 1-star and 3-star ones, not just 5-star
  • ✔ Ask how damage claims work — fakes have no answer
  • ✔ Confirm the truck number a day before the move

Already Scammed? Do This Now

  1. File a complaint at the National Consumer Helpline (1915) or edaakhil.nic.in
  2. File an FIR at your local police station with all payment proof
  3. Report the Google Business profile as fraudulent
  4. Warn others with an honest Google review describing exactly what happened
