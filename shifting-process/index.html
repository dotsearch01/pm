Our Shifting Process

From first call to final unpacking — here is exactly what happens when you move with Poonia Movers. No surprises, no hidden steps.

Step 1: Free Survey & Fixed Quote

Share your pickup and drop locations on call or WhatsApp. For homes above 2 BHK we do a free video survey — you walk us through your rooms on a video call. You get a written fixed quote the same day. What we quote is what you pay. 0% advance to confirm.

Step 2: 5-Layer Packing

On moving day our trained crew arrives with professional packing material: corrugated sheets, air-bubble wrap, foam edge guards, stretch film and waterproof lamination. TVs get custom wooden crating, mattresses get sealed covers. Every carton is labelled room-wise.

Step 3: Careful Loading

Heavy furniture goes first against the truck wall, anchored with straps. Fragile cartons ride on top, never crushed. Our crew chief photographs the loaded truck and shares it with you before the doors close.

Step 4: GPS-Tracked Transport

You get live GPS tracking on WhatsApp for the entire journey. The driver follows the planned route (NH-48 / NH-52 corridors for intercity moves) and our control room monitors progress. For intercity moves you also get the driver's direct number.

Step 5: Unloading & Unpacking

At destination, every carton is checked against the inventory list as it comes off the truck. Beds and basic furniture are reassembled free, cartons are unpacked on request, and packing debris is taken away. You sign off only when everything is in place.

What Makes Our Process Different

  • Written inventory of every item — signed by you and our crew chief
  • Photo documentation before packing and after loading
  • Single point of contact — one coordinator from booking to delivery
  • Damage protocol — any issue reported within 48 hours goes straight to our claim process
