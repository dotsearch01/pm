Cancellation & Refund Policy

Plans change — we get it. With 0% advance on most bookings, cancelling or rescheduling with Poonia Movers is simple and transparent.

1. Free Cancellation (0% Advance Bookings)

Most Poonia Movers bookings are confirmed with 0% advance payment. If you have paid no advance, you can cancel your booking free of charge any time before the truck and crew are dispatched — just call or WhatsApp us at +91 85292 06001.

2. Cancellation After Advance Payment

For intercity or large commercial moves where a token advance was taken:

  • More than 48 hours before scheduled move: 100% refund of advance.
  • 24–48 hours before scheduled move: 50% refund of advance (balance covers vehicle blocking).
  • Less than 24 hours / after truck dispatched: advance is non-refundable, as the vehicle and crew are already committed to your slot.

3. Free Rescheduling

Need a different date? Reschedule once free of charge up to 24 hours before your move, subject to slot availability. Additional reschedules may carry a nominal vehicle-holding charge, confirmed in writing before you agree.

4. Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to the original payment method (UPI / bank transfer). You will receive written confirmation on WhatsApp and email when the refund is initiated.

5. Service Delays & Our Guarantee

If Poonia Movers fails to arrive within the confirmed time window without prior notice, you are entitled to a 10% discount on the final bill or a free reschedule — your choice. Force-majeure events (extreme weather, road closures, government restrictions) are excluded.

6. Damage Claims

Damage claims are handled under our transit insurance process (coverage up to ₹10 lakh on insured moves). Report any damage within 48 hours of delivery with photos on WhatsApp for fastest resolution.
