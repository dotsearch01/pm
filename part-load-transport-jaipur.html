Part Load Transport: Clear Answers

Part-load (Less-Than-Truckload / LTL) transport allows multiple customers moving along the same highway route to share container space. You only pay for the exact volume (cubic feet) or weight (starting @ ₹4/Kg) occupied by your consignment instead of booking an entire truck.

Part-load rates start at ₹4 to ₹9 per kg for major North & Western India corridors (Delhi, Ahmedabad, Mumbai) and ₹8 to ₹14 per kg for Southern/Eastern routes (Bangalore, Hyderabad, Kolkata). Single household cartons and small appliances start at ₹450 per box.

Every part-load item is tagged with unique barcode serial stickers, packed in heavy-duty 5-layer corrugated wrap, and segregated inside the container using internal partition nets and pallet strapping to eliminate cargo shifting or mixing.

Part-load delivery from Jaipur takes 24 to 48 hours for Delhi NCR, 3 to 4 days for Mumbai and Ahmedabad, 5 to 7 days for Bangalore, Pune, and Hyderabad, and 6 to 8 days for Kolkata and Guwahati.
