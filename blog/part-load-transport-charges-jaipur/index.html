How Part Load Transport Works

  1. Booking: You book space for your consignment (e.g. 200 kg of cartons) — not the whole truck.
  2. Pickup: Goods are collected from your Jaipur location and brought to the consolidation hub.
  3. Consolidation: Your goods travel with other consignments heading the same direction — this sharing is what makes it cheap.
  4. Linehaul: The loaded truck runs the intercity route on a fixed schedule.
  5. Last-mile: At destination, goods are deconsolidated and delivered to the receiver's door.

Part Load vs Full Truck: Which Is Cheaper?

Rule of thumb: if your goods are under 1 ton (1,000 kg), part load is almost always cheaper. Above 3–4 tons, a full truck usually wins.

Example: 500 kg Jaipur to Mumbai by part load ≈ 500 × ₹5 = ₹2,500. A full 14ft Eicher for the same route ≈ ₹18,000+. You save over 85%.

The trade-off: part load takes 1–2 days longer (consolidation time) and involves more handling. For urgent or fragile high-value goods, full truck is better.

Booking Tips for Part Load

  • Pack in sturdy cartons with clear labels — your goods will be handled multiple times.
  • Weigh accurately; under-declared weight gets re-weighed and surcharged.
  • Ask about minimum chargeable weight (often 50–100 kg).
  • Confirm transit time in writing — part load runs on schedules, not on-demand.
  • Insure valuable consignments; basic carrier liability is limited.
