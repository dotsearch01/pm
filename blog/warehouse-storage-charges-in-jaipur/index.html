When Do You Need Storage?

  • Gap between houses: old lease ends before the new one starts — the most common reason
  • Renovation: keep furniture safe during home remodelling
  • Business inventory: e-commerce sellers and distributors storing stock near VKI/Sitapura industrial areas
  • Relocation abroad: store household goods during a 1–2 year posting
  • Seasonal goods: wedding/event equipment, winter stock

What Good Storage Security Looks Like

  • 24/7 CCTV with recording (not dummy cameras)
  • Fire hydrants and extinguishers — non-negotiable
  • Pest control schedule (ask for the last treatment date)
  • Elevated pallets — goods never sit directly on the floor (monsoon protection)
  • Moisture-proof wrapping on upholstery and mattresses
  • Digital inward/outward log with photos
  • Insurance option for stored goods

Detailed guide: How to Choose a Warehouse for Household Storage in Jaipur.

Questions to Ask Before Storing

  1. Is there a minimum lock-in period? (Avoid 6-month lock-ins for 1-month needs)
  2. How are goods packed before storage — and is packing charged extra?
  3. Can I visit/inspect my goods? How much notice?
  4. What happens in case of damage — is there a claim process?
  5. Are there separate bays or is everything in shared space?
  6. Is the monthly rent all-inclusive (no hidden 'handling' fees)?
